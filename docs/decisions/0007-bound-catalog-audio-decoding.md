# ADR-0007: Decode catalog audio through a supervised FFmpeg child

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Evidence refreshed:** 2026-10-15
- **Decision:** 2026-10-15; takes effect when this record is merged; implementation qualification remains incomplete
- **Issue:** [#118](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/118), parent [#114](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/114)

## Context

M5 proposes catalog tracks beside Lyria RealTime. A track would have to become the mixer's 48 kHz stereo signed 16-bit little-endian PCM without delaying its 20 ms reads. M4's ready-to-ship prerequisite was established by the [stable v1.0.0 delivery disposition](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/4#issuecomment-6097955604), with its original incomplete checks and specific maintainer exceptions retained. It is not a decoder, clean-host or broader NFR pass.

The [D1 research run](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/116#issuecomment-6104976415) completed all 48 cases and classifications, with 189 returned track appearances; 38 of all 42 music cases returned at least three tracks, while four returned none. On 13 October the maintainer [accepted the research direction qualitatively and waived structured ratings and a calculated go/no-go](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/116#issuecomment-6145275917), closing #116. The original deadline, 2026-10-12T23:40:29.544021Z, was missed and is not reset. Report-bound ratings remain null and the frozen summary remains `complete=false` / `recommendation=pending`; the mark of 28 of 42 music cases with three fitting tracks remains unmeasured. The first-ten weak-fit feedback belongs to #117/#126's quality follow-through, without invented scores.

D1's stream observations are HEAD metadata, not audio retrieval, decoding or playback. They do not establish bitstream format, sample rate/channels, completeness or suitability of the selected guardrails. The retained metadata already contains two track appearances above 900 seconds, two mp31 HEAD lengths above 16 MiB and one known mp32 length above 16 MiB; these potentially overlapping counts show sample exclusions, not a validated operating envelope. [The evidence report](../decoder-feasibility.md#research-disposition-and-catalog-technical-evidence) records the ranges and unavailable observations. Qualitative research acceptance does not approve a catalog's legal use, decoder, binary supplier, license exception or security change. This record accepts the decoder architecture on merge; the separate qualification gates below control runtime readiness and shipping.

The [earlier feasibility investigation](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/118#issuecomment-6032402289) covers the three candidate libraries/tools. The [offline evidence](../decoder-feasibility.md) distinguishes the original Windows control from the later selected minimal C1 Linux AMD64 assessment, relocation, source/notice inventory, limited resource observations and focused evaluator correction. Those completed assessments do not establish three-platform installation, ordinary source-mode containment, NFR-3 or distribution readiness; no Jamendo audio was decoded.

[ADR-0001](0001-build-on-python-discord-py-and-the-google-gen-ai-sdk.md) requires installation without a compiler and permissive dependencies. Both the inspected PyAV bundle and FFmpeg candidates introduce copyleft components. Their wrapper/build-script licenses do not license the bundled native code. [ADR-0004](0004-stream-lyria-realtime-through-buffered-decks.md) historically estimated up to three buffers during handover. Three nominal 60-second PCM payloads total 34,560,000 bytes; subtracting that from NFR-3's decimal 40 MB limit gives 5,440,000 bytes. This is historical nominal arithmetic, not demonstrated current worst-case headroom or an enforced runtime ceiling. Current producer admission does not enforce a three-buffer or aggregate PCM-byte ceiling, and Lyria's 60-second pause target can overshoot. A four-minute whole-track PCM decode needs approximately 46,080,000 bytes and exceeds the audio-buffer limit by itself.

## Requirements

1. [NFR-4, OPS-3 and OPS-4](../requirements.md): released, verified installation paths for Python 3.14 on Windows x86-64, Linux x86-64 and Linux ARM64, including the container, without a compiler on the operator's machine.
2. MP3 input, including 44.1 kHz mono/stereo, becomes exact 3,840-byte playback frames in the existing PCM format. Actual catalog bitstream formats remain a technical qualification gate because #116 used HEAD observations; an unsupported format fails one track.
3. The [mixer contract](../architecture.md#threads-and-buffers) remains intact. HTTP, native decoding, pipe I/O, process waiting and cleanup never run in `Mixer.read()`. Native decode work never runs on the event loop.
4. NFR-3 applies to resident audio buffers for a playing server, including compressed input, PCM copies and decoder audio buffering. Whole-process working set/commit and image size are separate measurements, not substitutes for this accounting.
5. Malformed input, stalls, cancellation and decoder exit fail one track and release its resources. A thread cannot contain a native crash. A subprocess can contain ordinary child failures, but resource limits and pipe-only arguments are not a security sandbox.
6. A verified binary-specific license, corresponding-source, vulnerability, update and footprint plan must precede distribution. This record defines a narrow native-component exception on merge; scanner settings require the exact verified component scope before any corresponding change.

## Options considered

| Option | Fit and operating burden | Unresolved adoption condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform FFmpeg package, separate process | Reuses the OS package manager on Linux; native decode stays out of Python. Additional Windows installation and child lifecycle handling are required. Distribution packages have larger dependency closures than the executable alone. | Not selected. Debian's default build includes GPL components; a different supply route would need a separate exact version, license/source, platform, footprint and update assessment. |
| Minimal C1 FFmpeg build, separate process | The selected approach builds on the M118-LGPL-9.0.2-C1 assessment: pinned upstream source, shared libraries and MP3/PCM/pipe capabilities with GPL/nonfree/version3/network disabled. Linux AMD64 behavior, corrected relocation and limited resource observations are recorded. | The LGPL-2.1-or-later supply contract is selected below. Exact shipped-runtime/source mapping, notices/SBOM, rebuild/replacement, other native targets and ordinary source containment still need qualification; no existing assessment artifact is approved for shipping. |
| Pinned prebuilt FFmpeg, separate process | Independent of Python ABI; the evaluated Windows shared build streamed MP3 successfully. Provider metadata lists Linux x86-64 and ARM64 artifacts. | The tested build reports LGPL-3.0-or-later, contains many unrelated libraries, and unpacks to 191 MB. Exact source/dependency inventory, retained supply, all-platform execution and memory enforcement remain unqualified. |
| PyAV or miniaudio in the bot | Python-facing streaming APIs simplify conversion, but a worker thread leaves native faults in the bot. | PyAV 19.0.1 has the required wheels but the inspected Linux bundle is GPL-3.0-or-later and about 131 MB unpacked. Miniaudio 1.71 has no released Linux ARM64 wheel. Neither satisfies the native-crash boundary in this form. Running either in a child would need a separately evaluated proposal and would retain its packaging/license obligations. |

The earlier investigation supplies the version-specific sources for the last row; it is not repeated here. A managed decoding service would add a service decision, transfer audio off-host and impose new operating obligations without solving this local need. No new service is proposed.

## Decision

Use **incremental FFmpeg decoding in a supervised child process**. The maintainer [selected this approach on 15 October](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/118#issuecomment-6193735484), including SobaFM-owned updates, required source/notices and supported-platform verification before shipping. This resolves the converter-route choice. It extends the [8 October assessment selection](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/118#issuecomment-6064977166); the original candidate survey is not reopened.

The remaining design selections here use the maintainer's [15 October delegated decision authority](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/114#issuecomment-6197383448). They define concrete work and qualification criteria without requesting the same choices again. Delegation does not supply missing platform, legal-compliance or resource evidence.

Select SobaFM-maintained minimal shared builds following the **M118-LGPL-9.0.2-C1 LGPL-2.1-or-later route**, with GPL/nonfree/version3/network disabled and only the required MP3-to-PCM/pipe capability. The tested C1 source/build is the qualification baseline, not a security guarantee for future releases. Broad distro GPL packages and the older Windows LGPLv3 control are not substitutes. The supply, containment, accounting and completion contracts below authorize implementation after #119 reconciles the accepted decisions. No current binary is qualified for distribution or catalog runtime readiness. The [recorded sequencing disposition](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/114#issuecomment-6198075027) separates acceptance of this design from qualification of its implementation.

On acceptance, this record narrowly supersedes ADR-0001's permissive-license requirement for the exact inventoried FFmpeg native runtime and its necessary licensed support components. Each shipped component must have a documented compatible distribution route; GPL-enabled, nonfree or unreviewed components are excluded. SobaFM's Python code remains MIT. This is not a general dependency allowlist exception, and accepted ADR-0001 is not edited by this proposal.

The application surface is a bounded catalog track producer that owns one child, feeds encoded chunks, drains output/errors concurrently, and hands frames through the existing frame-source interface. HTTP remains with the catalog client. There is no whole-track PCM result, decoder URL fetch, decoder-owned download, shell command construction or generic media pipeline.

### Engineering guardrails and readiness gates

Select the following upper engineering guardrails for implementation and qualification. They are not measured headroom, evidence of useful catalog coverage or a claim that the current Lyria runtime enforces them. The contract requires enforceable per-child resources, complete shared audio accounting and refusal of catalog readiness when either is unavailable. The implementation may allocate less within these ceilings to satisfy NFR-3; any higher ceiling requires a reviewed revision of the total budget. Catalog technical qualification must demonstrate a useful operating envelope before catalog readiness or shipping; #116's qualitative research disposition supplies no such result. This record does not reduce NFR-3 or change ADR-0004's generated-music behavior.

| Boundary | Selected guardrail and required enforcement | Failure behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Encoded input | 16 MiB cumulative per track, counted before each write regardless of `Content-Length`; at most 16 KiB per write, followed by flow-control drain. Retained encoded queue at most 256 KiB per child. | Reject advertised oversize early; abort on actual overrun. Do not retain the entire download. |
| Decode duration | At most 900 seconds, or 43,200,000 stereo sample frames, counted from output bytes divided by four. Metadata cannot extend the cap. | Reject overlong metadata; output crossing the cap fails the track rather than silently truncating it. |
| PCM delivery | Catalog reads at most 3,840 bytes; assemble across arbitrary short reads. Hard catalog-buffer ceiling of 60 seconds per deck and aggregate PCM ceiling of 34,560,000 bytes per server across every retained live, ended/retired spare, filling and incoming source in both modes. Neither is an existing runtime ceiling or a three-source rule; Lyria flow control requires the qualification below. | Backpressure the catalog producer when its reservation is full. `Mixer.read()` only pops ready frames or returns silence. Preserve current Lyria behavior; an unresolved bound refuses catalog readiness. |
| Parent audio memory | Allocate/reserve before retaining bytes. Ceiling 38,000,000 bytes per server across PCM, encoded queues, HTTP buffers, pipe transports, assembler tails and copies. Reserve each stdout/stderr/stdin transport independently within 256 KiB, including its qualified overshoot and in-flight storage; reject a configuration whose combined reservations exceed the ceiling. | Refuse an additional producer until resources are released. Queue length alone is insufficient; account for byte lengths and copies. |
| Native audio memory | An audited maximum native audio-buffer reservation must also fit strictly below 40,000,000 bytes with the parent reservation. If it exceeds the remaining headroom, lower the actual parent/PCM allocation before catalog readiness. | An unknown bound refuses catalog readiness. The scratch working-set observation does not establish a native audio-buffer bound. |
| Child process resources | 128 MiB per child and at most two children per server/four globally. Use a separate Linux cgroup with `memory.max` and `memory.swap.max=0`, or a Windows Job Object with process/job memory limits and kill-on-close, established before untrusted bytes enter the child. | A cap breach fails that child/track. A bot-wide container limit alone can kill the bot and does not meet the containment boundary. |
| Diagnostics | Concurrent stderr draining; retain at most 4 KiB, with a separately bounded reader transport. Record a fixed error category and exit status, never raw native diagnostics or URLs in user output/logs. | A noisy child cannot fill an unbounded diagnostic result or block stdout. |
| Time | 15 seconds to first PCM; 15 seconds without input/output progress while work is expected, excluding intentional queue backpressure; absolute lifetime at most `2 * 900 + 30` seconds, including pauses. | Timeout fails one track. Each input/pipe operation remains cancellable; a pause never disables the absolute lifetime cap. |
| Stop/cancel | One cleanup owner has a three-second total deadline from the first cleanup request, covering feeding cancellation, stdin closure, every owned task join, bounded stdout/stderr draining to EOF, transport closure and process-tree reaping. Drain concurrently with termination; wait up to one second after terminate, then kill if needed and wait up to one further second, all within that same deadline. | Any unfinished resource at the deadline is cleanup failure, even if the direct child has exited. Retain its cleanup debt, audio reservations and child slot; admit no replacement against that capacity until actual release is verified. Repeated cancellation cannot interrupt the owner or reset its deadline. |

The three-second total is a selected implementation requirement to qualify on every supported installation, not a measured guarantee from the scratch harness. Begin pipe draining/discarding while stopping the feeder and terminating the process tree; do not wait for task joins or child exit before draining. All closure, drain and join waits share the same monotonic deadline rather than receiving fresh timeouts. Reaping the direct child does not prove EOF or closure: descendants can inherit pipe handles. If EOF, task completion, transport closure or tree reaping is still unverified at the deadline, return a cleanup failure and retain a tracked cleanup owner for the outstanding debt. Keep every reservation and child slot needed by unreleased resources charged across station replacement; neither direct-child exit nor the deadline refunds them. Later supervised cleanup may release them only after verifying actual closure/reaping and release of retained storage.

A count of open producers does not account for all retained audio. The [supplemental independent review](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/pull/157#pullrequestreview-5449091514), following [the sibling proposal's finding](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/pull/156#pullrequestreview-5448974590), reproduced four nonempty Lyria buffers: an ended live source with 8 seconds, an ended ready spare with 60 seconds, and two replacement producers with 2 seconds each. `Station._generate()` counts open producers; `Station._discard()` can preserve that spare while neither replacement is ready. The fixture's 72 seconds total 13,824,000 PCM bytes: it disproves a hard three-buffer assumption but does not demonstrate an NFR-3 overrun or establish worst-case headroom.

Qualification of the shared byte envelope must count every retained live, spare, retired/ended, filling and incoming source, regardless of whether its producer is open or which mode owns it. Include Lyria receive overshoot and in-flight chunks/copies through rotation, plan replacement, recovery, voice pauses and both directions of mode transitions. A byte envelope can admit four partially filled buffers; it does not impose an unconditional three-source ceiling. Coordinate admission/accounting with ADR-0006 in [#156](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/pull/156), preserving current Lyria generation/spare-retention behavior and its tests. If that behavior cannot fit, keep catalog unavailable and record a reviewed design revision under the delegated decision authority; do not silently drop a spare, restrict replacement generation or change NFR-3. The numeric envelope and native/transport reservations remain unqualified.

Use the [reviewed shared accounting contract](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/118#issuecomment-6193982126): one station-owned byte ledger, source-owned allocation leases and a cleanup lease that survives caller cancellation and station replacement. Reserve each allocation, copy and qualified in-flight increment before retention. Count a backing allocation once when aliased, but charge separate copies separately. The invariant is all attributable resident audio storage below 40,000,000 bytes per server, including conservatively reserved transport/native storage. Catalog admission must reserve reachable future Lyria growth while catalog audio can remain retained, rather than subtracting only present queue lengths. Cleanup releases capacity only after tasks, transports, process trees and retained audio have actually released it; producer-capacity reuse does not release the station's Lyria process-pool reservation. No source-only hard Lyria maximum or nonzero catalog allowance is established yet. This is a proof gap, not a newly observed NFR-3 overrun.

Asyncio's reader `limit` is a flow-control threshold, not an exact memory allocation ceiling: the experiment with a 16 KiB limit observed a 52,508-byte stdout reader buffer. Transport reservations must include overshoot and in-flight chunks and be qualified against the chosen Python/build/platform versions. A reservation policy that cannot bound its transports must fail qualification.

Require a separately enforceable OS boundary before untrusted bytes reach a child: Linux cgroup v2 with delegated access for the unprivileged bot, or a Windows Job Object with verified assignment, memory and tree-cleanup semantics. [Linux cgroup documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/admin-guide/cgroup-v2.html) and [Windows Job Objects](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/procthread/job-objects) describe different accounting and enforcement semantics. These are selected mechanisms to qualify, not claims that the experiment exercised them. Missing executable, delegation, assignment or enforcement capability refuses catalog readiness with actionable setup guidance; Lyria availability retains its existing behavior. Qualification must prove launch/assignment ordering, cap breach and tree cleanup under actual compiler-free source/container installations. No runtime privilege increase, bot-wide-container substitute or silent RSS-polling fallback is allowed. Linux AMD64, native ARM64 and Windows x86-64 all remain required targets.

### Input, output and process contract

Use an absolute verified executable and fixed argument array with `asyncio.create_subprocess_exec`; inherit only the OS environment needed to launch it. Neither credentials nor source URLs enter arguments or the child environment. Force `-f mp3`, whitelist `pipe`, select `-map 0:a:0`, suppress other stream types, and explicitly emit `pcm_s16le`, two channels and 48 kHz to `pipe:1`. The catalog client owns URL validation and HTTP completion. [`-max_alloc`](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html) limits one native allocation, not total child memory; it cannot replace the supervisor limit. The evaluated command is in the evidence report.

A nonzero exit, broken pipe, sample cap, missing/unaligned output, timeout or incomplete HTTP transfer is a track error. Zero exit alone is insufficient: the synthetic truncation test emitted only 1.96 seconds of a 240-second track and exited zero. Select strict completion: require a finite positive validated catalog duration within the adopted duration cap, clean HTTP completion, matching declared length when available, a zero decoder exit and sample-aligned output. Reject missing/invalid duration rather than treating it as an unrestricted stream. Compare decoded duration with catalog duration using a one-second absolute allowance for rounded metadata and codec padding; that chosen allowance still needs technical qualification against actual supported input. Exceeding it fails the track, including a zero-exit truncation.

A catalog source is ready with at least six seconds of reserved PCM. A shorter source is ready only after clean HTTP completion, bounded stdout/stderr draining to EOF, zero exit/reaping and every strict completion check have verified its complete shorter output. EOF alone is not readiness or success. Normal production completes only after that verification; the performance finishes only when its buffered PCM has also drained. Ordinary short reads, duration agreement or a direct-child exit cannot stand in for these conditions. A streaming source may reach the six-second readiness threshold before full-track verification, so late failure remains possible.

A failure before audible handover leaves the heard source under the existing fallback rules. A failure discovered after a streamed prefix was heard is still a failed track: retire production, retain audio/cleanup ownership until release, and let the catalog engine recover to the next eligible source under its bounded failure policy. Never report that track complete or count ordinary catalog EOF as a Lyria failure. #119/#125/#127 must carry these outcomes through source ownership and listener feedback; streaming cannot promise retrospective removal of a heard prefix.

At verified normal EOF, a tail aligned to four bytes may be zero-padded once to a 3,840-byte frame, charged to the byte/sample reservations. Padding cannot extend the adopted cap. Compare duration using decoded samples before this playback-frame padding. An incomplete stereo sample is an error. Encoder delay/padding caused both successful fixtures to end between playback-frame boundaries; reads must not be assumed frame-aligned. These contracts belong in #119 before #125/#127 implementation; this record changes no current station behavior.

Follow the [selected successive-playback policy](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/117#issuecomment-6197676011) in ADR-0006: every boundary involving catalog audio ends the outgoing performance before the incoming source begins, with no catalog fade or overlap. Readiness permits a prepared replacement to wait for natural completion or an explicit skip/replacement; it does not authorize an early crossfade. Generated-to-generated transitions keep their existing behavior. Preserve the hour-long playback, at-most-two-second underrun and other unaffected requirements for later qualification.

[Python's subprocess documentation](https://docs.python.org/3.14/library/asyncio-subprocess.html) requires Windows Proactor support and warns that `communicate()` retains output in memory. Concurrent bounded readers and explicit task ownership are required instead. Successful child reaping alone does not prove parent pipe closure; the scratch harness initially exposed unclosed transports before its cleanup was corrected.

### Binary license, source and upkeep obligations

The build supplier's MIT scripts and a permissive Python wrapper do not override FFmpeg or linked-library licenses. FFmpeg's [released license inventory](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/n9.0.2/LICENSE.md) distinguishes LGPL, GPL, version-3 and nonfree configurations. The original Windows control's `-L` reports LGPL version 3 or later; `--enable-version3` is present, and `--enable-gpl`/`--enable-nonfree` are absent. The selected minimal route instead targets LGPL-2.1-or-later with GPL/nonfree/version3 disabled. Its [reviewed inventory](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/118#issuecomment-6068002879) still includes IJG/ISC/MIT/zlib-derived material and compiler/runtime support. Neither assessment record establishes complete license compliance.

The SobaFM release maintainer, currently [@slackysoba](https://github.com/slackysoba), owns the native distribution manifest, update response and durable source/notices access; contributors may prepare and independently review those artifacts. Reuse the project's existing GitHub releases and image registry, with no new paid service. Provide versioned compiler-free Linux AMD64/ARM64 and Windows x86-64 runtime packages from the same reviewed minimal-build recipe, and include only the qualified runtime closure in images. Pin source, toolchain, build configuration and resulting artifacts by digest. An arbitrary executable found on `PATH`, a mutable daily archive or a wider system package is not an approved substitute. Operators may replace/rebuild the separately installed native component under the documented mechanism; a replacement must pass the same readiness checks before catalog playback.

Before any binary enters a SobaFM image or download, the chosen-build record must contain:

1. Exact artifact/version/architecture hashes, supplier and upstream commit, complete configure line, patches, dependency versions/licenses and a reproducible build recipe. Inventory every redistributed DLL/static component; exclude nonfree builds. Preserve copyright, license and required attribution notices in the image and accompanying documentation.
2. Complete corresponding source for each covered redistributed component, including modifications and build/install scripts. Select direct source delivery/access under [LGPL-2.1 sections 4-6](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/n9.0.2/COPYING.LGPLv2.1), rather than a written offer. Publish hash-pinned source/notices bundles in the same existing GitHub release as the native downloads and binary/image manifest; map every image digest and platform package to the exact bundle. Verify that each distribution surface supplies the access or accompanying source its component licenses require before publication. Keep those bundles available while their binaries remain obtainable, with no routine deletion of published bundles. A moving upstream link, daily archive or source tag without covered dependency sources is insufficient. Retention is the selected project policy, not an assertion that every license universally requires three years or the complete compiler source tree.
3. For applicable LGPL combined works, notices plus a demonstrated shared-library replacement/relinking mechanism or required relinkable material; provide installation information where the component's license requires it. The package must preserve shared-library loading and supply the exact recipe and materials needed to rebuild/replace covered native code; test recipient replacement separately from SobaFM's normal build. FFmpeg's [legal checklist](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html) is a useful additional linking checklist. A pipe boundary is a technical boundary; the actual component/distribution assessment must be recorded and independently reviewed. This decision does not pronounce every aggregation/linking arrangement MIT-compatible.
4. Review native-component advisories at least monthly and on upstream security notices; the release maintainer triages applicable fixes within two working days. Suspend catalog decoding when an applicable serious defect has no approved mitigation, rather than keeping an unsafe package ready. Every update needs refreshed hashes/source/licenses/SBOM, all supported platform/cancellation checks and footprint comparison. A Python lockfile scan alone does not inventory bundled native code; use the [FFmpeg security history](https://ffmpeg.org/security.html) and advisories for included libraries.

The C1 source/notice inventory retains matching GCC/glibc original and Debian patch archives, but descriptor signatures, installed source-to-object correspondence, full shipped-runtime SBOM and recipient rebuild/replacement remain unverified. Applicable obligations must be determined component by component; source inventories alone do not prove object correspondence or complete compiler-source duties. The release manifest must record native scanner coverage and gaps, source accessibility and the verified policy scope. BtbN's [retention policy](https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/blob/9acad4a9ef1583096af7836cc1e9c8cbcb4d3950/README.md) keeps ordinary daily builds only fourteen days, so the evaluated dated download is unsuitable as the durable supply/source record. These selected responsibilities do not create a package, publish source or change the current allowlist.

## Consequences

Acceptance on merge settles the architecture, selected guardrails and ownership; it authorizes implementation after #119 records the corresponding requirements. Qualification of that implementation is mandatory before catalog readiness or shipping, rather than a circular prerequisite to accepting this design. The offline results show incremental streaming and recovery on the tested paths, not release readiness. Additional binary installation, supervision and distribution upkeep remain real responsibilities for operators and maintainers.

The primary tracks the following work as explicit dependencies under [#114](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/114), preserving #116's completed qualitative disposition, frozen D1 evidence and missed deadline. No invented rating or unchanged provider run is needed for this decision.

- **Requirements owner, #119:** reconcile this record and ADR-0006 into identified M5 requirements and architecture before dependent runtime implementation. Record the shared accounting, fail-closed readiness, strict completion and successive catalog boundary contracts, including the explicit supersession of catalog/cross-mode crossfades.
- **Native supply owner and release maintainer, [#174](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/174):** prepare the pinned minimal shared compiler-free packages and qualify exact shipped-runtime/source correspondence, SBOM/notices, independent recipient rebuild/replacement, source access on each binary/image distribution surface, scanner coverage and update response. This follow-on depends on #118/#119 and gates #125's production readiness and #132's shipping. Reuse retained C1 inventory; the older Windows LGPLv3 control is a different build. The narrow exception defined here does not broaden the Python allowlist or edit accepted ADR-0001.
- **Shared source/station owners, #120/#121:** implement and qualify the station-owned ledger, reachable Lyria retention/receive/copy obligation, parent transport/native reservations and cleanup debt. Include deterministic cases for ended live plus spare plus two replacements, delayed/failed pause, prolonged voice pause, copy versus alias, in-flight mixer ownership and debt across station replacement. Preserve existing Lyria behavior and NFR-3. No nonzero catalog allocation is qualified yet; that gap blocks catalog readiness, not work to implement the ledger.
- **Track producer and catalog owners, #124/#125/#127:** implement owned HTTP boundaries, bounded production, strict completion, prefetch/sequence admission and bounded recovery. Verify missing duration, the one-second allowance, truncated zero-exit input, arbitrary pipe splits, final padding, six-second/short-source readiness, repeated cancellation and whole-operation cleanup failure. Establish actual supported bitstream formats, sample rates/channels, encoded sizes and completion behavior through bounded in-memory qualification. D1 HEAD successes and missing lengths do not supply those guarantees.
- **Supported-platform qualification owner, [#175](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/175):** verify compiler-free clean installation, pre-input resource enforcement, the three-second cleanup contract and inherited-pipe/tree debt under actual unprivileged source/container installations, with audited native audio and parent/transport accounting. Linux AMD64 assessment observations are complete within their recorded scope; native Linux ARM64 and selected C1 Windows qualification remain open. This follow-on depends on #119/#121/#124/#125/#174 and relevant #127/#128 integration; it gates #131/#132 and catalog readiness. An aggregate container cap is insufficient. Reuse completed observations rather than repeating unchanged builds, smoke tests or general controls.
- **Operational and release owners, #130/#131/#132:** verify the clean-machine guidance, catalog successive playback and generated-behavior preservation, at-most-two-second underrun over an hour and all other unaffected milestone criteria. No binary/image publication or catalog shipping precedes the source/distribution and supported-platform gates above.

The implementation issues become eligible only after their accepted-decision, #119 and other issue dependencies are met. Catalog mode stays unavailable while any applicable supply, containment, accounting or completion qualification is unmet; generated availability retains its existing behavior. [Issue #118](https://github.com/slackysoba/sobafm/issues/118) closes when this reviewed decision is accepted, with the unfinished implementation qualifications transferred to their tracked owners. The `maintainer-approval` label requires acceptance of this decision record; the maintainer's delegated authority resolves the outstanding design choices without a further approval question. This documentation change adds no runtime, dependency, container or scanner change.

## Revisit triggers

- Catalog technical qualification finds non-MP3 formats or durations/sizes outside agreed caps, or quality follow-through contradicts the accepted research premise.
- A candidate's native audio memory or image footprint cannot meet the approved budget, or supervision cannot work on a supported installation.
- License/source obligations cannot be satisfied, the supplier removes retained artifacts, or an applicable parser vulnerability cannot be mitigated promptly.
- A permissively licensed candidate gains complete platform packaging and independently verified child-process containment with a smaller operating burden.
